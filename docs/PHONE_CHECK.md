# Phone check – 10 minutes on every new app version

The computer tests can't hear sounds, lock the screen or walk outside. These checks can.
Do them after installing a new version. You need: AirPods or the speaker, music, 10 minutes.
Write down the number of anything that fails and send it to Claude (or use Report a problem in Settings, once it exists).

## Before you start (1 min)
1. Open the app. It starts on the beating heart, then Home. **Nothing black, nothing stuck.**
2. Profile › scroll to the bottom: the version shows the new date.
3. Your workouts and runs from before are still there (Home, History).

## Sounds over music, screen locked (3 min)
4. Play music (Spotify / Apple Music).
5. Train › Strength › Empty workout. Add any exercise, type a weight and reps, tick ✓.
   The rest timer starts. **Music keeps playing.**
6. Lock the phone. When the rest ends you hear the **ding** over the music (and a light buzz).
7. Unlock: the **lock-screen card** showed the workout while locked (name, set, clock).
8. On the lock-screen card tap **Set done** (needs iOS 17 or newer): the next set gets ticked.
9. Add a timed exercise (Plank), press ▶, lock the phone: **beep at the end**, rest, the next hold starts by itself.

## Heart-rate zone beeps (2 min)
10. Train › Strength › Empty workout › pick a heart-rate zone (make one if you have none,
    e.g. "Test" 120–140). Monitoring starts by itself.
11. Home › Tools › Heart-rate zones: move the simulated slider above the zone (or use your strap),
    lock the phone: after ~5 s **3 high beeps** over the music. Below the zone: **2 low beeps**.
12. Go back to the workout and Finish (or Discard). **The beeps stop** – monitoring switches off
    by itself because the workout started it.

## A short run outside (3 min – a walk is fine)
13. Train › Run › Ready sheet: zone, voice on › Start. Hear **3-2-1**, feel a buzz.
14. Lock the phone and walk 2–3 minutes. The **blue location pill** shows at the top.
15. Pause, then Resume: you hear "Paused" / "Resumed".
16. Finish › Save. The summary shows the **map with your route**, distance, splits.
17. Music came back to full volume after each spoken update.

## Data safety (1 min)
18. Profile › Your data › Back up → save to Files. The file appears in Files.
19. (Only once in a while) Restore that file: everything is still there afterwards.

## If something fails
- Write the step number + what happened ("11 – no beep when locked").
- A screen recording helps a lot (Control Centre › Screen Recording).
