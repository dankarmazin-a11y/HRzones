Line figures, round 4

Line figures from real 3D bodies (your pick). Each exercise now gets the camera angle that shows its movement best, with the same body size and floor for all. Benches, seats and pull-up bars are drawn where the body rests or hangs, and plates are real discs. Light mode is style B, dark mode style C.

Camera per exercise

Front-to-back movements (squat, curl, push-up) are shown from the side. Movements out to the sides (lateral raise) get a front three-quarter view. Everyone faces the same way.

Benches and bars

A bench appears under the shoulders on hip thrusts, a seat on seated presses and a bar above the hands on pull-ups. Plates have thickness, a lighter face and a centre hole.
