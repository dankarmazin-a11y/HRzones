The camera is fixed for each exercise; only the body moves. Feet that stand stay planted, hands stay on the bar, the floor or the pull-up bar, and parts that do not work in the exercise hold still. Main muscle in orange, secondary in light orange. Light mode is style B, dark mode style C.

Planted and holding

A foot on the floor in both photos is pinned and the leg bends to reach it. Hands on the floor or on a pull-up bar are pinned the same way. Nothing goes through the floor.

Barbell

One grip width for the whole movement, both hands exactly on the bar, and the bar stays level. It sits on the upper back, the front of the shoulders, the hips or in the hands, by exercise.

No tilting, no extra movement

The shoulders and hips are levelled left-to-right (small tilts under 25° are photo or model errors). The two photos are lined up on what touches the floor or pad in both, so the body doesn't drift. Parts that turn less than 6° stay still; idle legs hold one pose, and on hanging exercises they hang in line with the body with soft knees.
